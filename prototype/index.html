How to read this. Evidence tags: Verified primary record, Reported news or official post, Estimated our calculation, Illustrative a stand-in value that shows the method until a survey measures it.
The cross-sections are conceptual. Road heights, culvert sizes and flood flows are illustrative until topographic survey, LiDAR and hydrology are done; the mechanism (culvert inlet and orifice flow, road overtopping as a weir, ice-jam stage for Kashechewan) and the sizing method are real. Costs are AACE Class 5 order-of-magnitude estimates in 2026 CAD built from Alberta Transportation 2026 unit price averages; schedules are planning estimates from a hypothetical January 2027 start, not delivery dates.
