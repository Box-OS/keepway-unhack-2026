## B. Data items

### 1. Highway 918
- Length 92 km, Hwy 165 near Beauval to Patuanak (Fisher Ave). Source: Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saskatchewan_Highway_918 . Reported (tertiary).
- Hansard 2026-05-07 gives "87-kilometre stretch". Verified primary as a quote, but it is a politician's figure. Treat the length as 87 to 92 km.
- Route follows the Beaver River, the east shore of Lac Île-à-la-Crosse, and the west shore of Shagwenaw Lake (Wikipedia).
- Surface type (gravel?): **not found in a primary source.** 900-series Saskatchewan highways are typically gravel or thin-membrane northern roads. Estimated, unverified.
- Width / design class: **not found.**
- AADT for Hwy 918: **not found.** For context, MHI AADT on adjacent Hwy 165 segments, from the Denison Wheeler River EA memo (2022-04-20, MHI data 2011-2019, https://iaac-aeic.gc.ca/050/documents/p80178/145557E.pdf): Hwy 165 from Hwy 914 to Hwy 918 averaged 250 AADT (180 in 2018-19), truck AADT 37. Hwy 165 from Hwy 918 to Hwy 155 averaged 842 AADT (780 in 2019), truck AADT 61. Verified primary (MHI data via EA). These are upper bounds; 918 is a dead-end spur, so it would carry less.
- Hwy 165 near Hwy 918 junction also washed out (paNOW 2026-05-02). Culvert washout between Beauval and Beauval Commercial Junction, replaced by MHI crews (paNOW 2026-05-03).

### 2. Population
- Patuanak (northern hamlet), Census 2021: 63 (2016: 73). Wikipedia/StatCan https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patuanak . Reported, from StatCan.
- Wapachewunak 192D (main Patuanak reserve), Census 2021: about 565 (310 male + 255 female). StatCan estimates: 568 (2022), 562 (2025). https://www.citypopulation.de/en/canada/saskatchewan/admin/division_no_18/4718814__wapachewunak_192d . Reported, from StatCan. Census 2011: 482.
- La Plonge 192 (near Beauval; also served via Hwy 165/918 corridor). Census 2011: 115. The CBC fire-ban story refers to "residents of Patuanak and La Plonge".
- ERFN registered membership: 1,839 (846 on-reserve, 983 off-reserve), April 2024, City of Saskatoon First Nation profile, sourced from ISC (https://saskatoon.ca/sites/default/files/documents/community-services/planning-development/future-growth/urban-reserves-treaty-land-entitlement/FNP_EnglishRiver.pdf). Reported. Other figures: 1,747 (2022, Gladue USask); 1,625 (2019, Wikipedia). Band number 400.
- Reserves: 19 listed (La Plonge 192, Elak Dase 192A, Knee Lake 192B, Dipper Rapids 192C, Wapachewunak 192D, Île-à-la-Crosse 192E, Primeau Lake 192F, Cree Lake 192G, English River 192H, Barkwell Bay 192I, Grasswoods 192J, Haultain Lake 192K, Flatstone Lake 192L, Cable Bay 192M/N, Beauval Forks 192O, Leaf Rapids 192P, Slush Lake 192Q, Mawdsley Lake 192R). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_River_First_Nation
- July 2025 wildfire: "700 people stranded in northern Sask. community" (CBC headline; it surfaced in Patuanak searches, but I have not confirmed that the community is Patuanak, https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/saskatchewan/wildfire-northern-stranded-highway-blocked-1.7577647).

### 3. Terrain / elevation
- Churchill River near Patuanak (WSC 06BB003) water level: 2025 daily means 418.75 to 419.25 m. Max daily 419.254 m on 2025-06-07; Dec 2025 about 418.77 m. Geodetic Survey of Canada datum. Verified primary (ECCC/WSA API). So river or lake level at Patuanak is about 419 m ASL.
- Patuanak Airport (CKB2) elevation 1,426 to 1,431 ft, about 435 to 436 m ASL. Beauval Airport (CJK3) 1,430 to 1,436 ft, about 436 m. Reported (aviation databases; confirm against the CFS). The whole corridor is therefore low relief, about 420 to 440+ m. The "Patuanak ~420 m" claim fits the waterline; the townsite and airport sit about 435 m.
- Land cover: boreal forest with muskeg, heavily burned by the 2025 wildfire. Candyce Paul (CBC/TWN 2026-05-04): snow fell on burned land with "no living trees, root systems or muskeg to absorb" meltwater, and "new lakes and rivers" formed along the road. Reported. The Churchill River Upland / Canadian Shield transition lies near Patuanak. Ecoregion assignment is Estimated, not verified.
- Creek names at the 17/29/40 km washouts: **not found.**

### 6. Prior history on Hwy 918
- July 25, 2020: torrential rain caused two washouts, about 60 km and about 72 km N of the Hwy 165 junction. Temporary detour built; reopened Mon July 27; closed again July 28 by rising water. Councillor Katrina Maurice: washouts "almost every year, ranging from minor to extreme"; Beaver River high. MHI (Steve Shaheen): permanent fix waits for water to recede. Northern Pride Meadow Lake, 2020-07-29 https://northernprideml.com/?p=7062 . Reported.
- July 2025: wildfire (Muskeg fire) closed Hwy 918. State of emergency; about 700 stranded, residents flown out; 141 evacuated from La Plonge. https://larongenow.com/2025/07/09/state-of-emergency-declared-for-patuanak/ ; CBC. Reported.
- 2011, 2014, 2022 events on 918: **not found.**

### 7. Additional 2026 details
- 2026-05-01 (Fri): closure about 5:15 p.m.; water had been over the road for 2 days (Paul, CBC snippet). Lakes and rivers still frozen, so no boat access. The airstrip stayed open to small planes for supplies.
- 2026-05-02: CBC says crews were replacing a culvert. paNOW lists Hwy 165 and 918 near Beauval closed by washout with local detours.
- 2026-05-03: closures at 17, 29 and 40 km (paNOW). CBC says "at least three active closures" and "dozens" of ERFN people displaced.
- 2026-05-04: TWN/CBC reports about 6 washout places, about 160 stranded outside, and burn debris blocking culverts. Minister Gartner said crews were assessing damage, repairing roads and keeping culverts open. MBC says 918 still closed.
- 2026-05-05 Hansard QP: Jordan McPhail (NDP, Cumberland): "The road into Patuanak is washed out due to flooding and, as of this morning, Highway 918 is closed. And this government cut $36 million for northern roads..." Hon. Kim Gartner: "crews are working tirelessly assessing road damage, repairing roadways, and working with community leaders..." https://docs.legassembly.sk.ca/legdocs/Assembly/Debates/30L2S/20260505DebatesHTML.htm
- 2026-05-05 paNOW: still closed at 8 p.m. Monday; culverts being installed; gravel stockpiled; water "appears to be subsiding" (ERFN Management Team).
- 2026-05-06 GX94: McPhail says "The lake has not completely thawed out yet, so we can't use the water on waterways"; restoration timelines "need to be clearly communicated".
- 2026-05-07 Hansard: "five different places", "87-kilometre stretch", "No way in and no way out". People were told to go to Île-à-la-Crosse in an emergency, 165 km away. A speaker also said the road should be double-lane by now (search snippet).
- About 2026-05-07 (Thu night): travel permitted with "extreme caution" (CBC snippet). The fire ban came the following Sunday after a man-made fire.
- Regional: Hwy 914 closed about 17 km N of Pinehouse; Smoothstone River bridge on Hwy 165 collapsed, isolating Pinehouse (CBC); Hwy 908 Île-à-la-Crosse closed May 8 for a new culvert (meadowlakeNOW 2026-05-10).
