Engineering-judgment schedule assumptions (Estimated):
- Feasibility + site investigation 3–6 months; geotechnical drilling in the north needs frozen-ground or summer access windows.
- Community engagement runs in parallel from day 1 and must conclude (Band Council Resolution / community approval) before funding application.
- Hydrologic/hydraulic modelling 2–4 months after survey data; ice-jam modelling (Kashechewan) 4–8 months and needs at least one breakup season of observation.
- Funding approval cycles: federal/provincial programs typically annual intake; 4–12 months.
- Construction seasons: Kashechewan ~4.5 months (mid-June–Oct; winter-road delivery of heavy equipment/materials Jan–Mar); NW Saskatchewan ~5 months (mid-May to mid-Oct, after spring breakup/road bans); Manitoba Parkland ~6 months (May–Oct); Cape Breton ~7 months (Apr–Nov); in-water work windows for fish further restrict culvert work (typically ~Jun 1–Sep 30 in NS; ~Jul 15–Sep 30 / Jun 15–Mar 31 varies by species in prairies).
