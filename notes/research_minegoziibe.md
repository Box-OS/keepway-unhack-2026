## B. Population
- Pine Creek FN (Minegoziibe), 2021: on reserve 648; on other land 320; off reserve 2,912; total 3,880 (registered). Reported (Wikipedia infobox citing ISC First Nation Profiles). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine_Creek_First_Nation
- Alternatives: ISC QP note gives 637 on-reserve and 3,774 total (date unclear) https://search.open.canada.ca/qpnotes/record/isc-sac%2CISC-2020-10049 ; CCMB profile gives 700 (2021 Census) https://ccmbindigenouscommunityprofiles.ca/community/pine-creek-first-nation ; FN website gives registered 3,170 with about 1,200 on reserve (older) https://pinecreekfirstnation.com/location/ . An older CBC flood story says "about 1,100 residents".
- Camperville (Northern Affairs Community), 2021 Census (revised 2023): 424 people, 174 of 176 private dwellings, 8.7 km². 2016: 487. 2011: 547. Verified primary (Manitoba INR community profile, StatCan). https://www.gov.mb.ca/inr/publications/community_profiles/pubs/camperville.pdf ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camperville,_Manitoba
- Camperville coordinates: 51.988 N, 100.143 W (Wikipedia).
- Swan River town: >5,000 (CBC 2 Jul).

## C. Cause: rainfall and hydrology
- Two storm periods: June 6–8 and June 28–July 1, 2026 (Manitoba DFA programs). Manitoba release, 10 Jul 2026: "over 200 millimetres of rain" fell in western Manitoba/Parkland between June 6 and July 1. Verified primary. https://news.gov.mb.ca/news/print,index.html?item=74497&posted=2026-07-10
- Dauphin: "just shy of 119 mm" over 5 days to 2 Jul 2026 (ECCC meteorologist Terri Lang). Normal for June is about 80 mm. Reported. https://globalnews.ca/news/11950514/flooding-western-manitoba/
- Dauphin (City): 115 mm since the evening of June 28; local SOE July 1. Verified primary (municipal). https://www.dauphin.ca/p/flooding
- Dauphin June 2026 total: just over 211 mm, 2nd-wettest June on record. Reported (CBC). https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/9.7255193
- Swan River: about 107 mm "in recent days" (to 2 Jul). Reported (Global/ECCC). June total nearly 270 mm (ECCC, via CBC). https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/manitoba/swan-river-flooding-community-9.7257385
- Swan River level about 3 m above normal (mayor's estimate, 2 Jul). Reported.
- Camperville/Pine Creek station rainfall: not found.
- Pine River / Pine Creek WSC gauge: not found for 2026. The historical WSC station 05LG001 "North Pine River near Pine River" (51.81 N, 100.53 W, 210 km² drainage, record 1954–1994, discontinued) is listed at https://www.r-arcticnet.sr.unh.edu/Points/P2806.html . I could not confirm a 05LE station for Pine River. No 2026 peak flows found.
- Lake Winnipegosis level: no 2026 reading extracted (the MB lake-level PDF did not parse). The latest found is 830.95 ft (about 253.27 m) at Winnipegosis on 1 May 2025 (MB HFC). https://www.gov.mb.ca/floodinfo/lake_levels/lake_winnipegosis.pdf . The ~254 m database assumption is roughly consistent (Estimated).

## D. Road failures (Manitoba Transportation and Infrastructure lists via media)
- 2 Jul 2026 (MTI list as of 04:36), PortageOnline https://portageonline.com/articles/dozens-of-manitoba-highways-remain-closed-flooding-continues-in-parkland :
  - Hwy 20: Hwy 271 to Camperville (new); Hwy 271 to Road 182N (Winnipegosis); Hwy 20A to Hwy 267 (local traffic).
  - Hwy 10: Pine River to Cowan closed (photo of washout near Pine River); washed out 10 km east of Swan River; Hwy 488 to Minitonas (Hwy 366); Steep Rock River bridge north of Mafeking; 5 km south of Mafeking; Bellsite to Hwy 77; Ashville north jct to Grandview; and others.
  - Detour to Swan River via Hwy 268 through Birch River.
  - All roads into Duck Mountain PP closed (Hwy 366, 367 segments).
  - Up to 50 provincial roads damaged; up to 200 mm of rain.

## F. Costs and assistance
- $5M DFA advance for municipalities (late June–July flood), on top of about $18M from the June flooding (Premier Kinew, CBC 2 Jul). Reported.
- MFRP (10 Jul 2026): one-time grant; eligible areas include the "Northern Affairs Community of Camperville" (Minegoziibe is not listed; First Nations are typically covered via ISC/EMAP). Red Cross donation match up to $15M. Verified primary. https://news.gov.mb.ca/news/print,index.html?item=74497&posted=2026-07-10
- $15M allocated to the one-time grant; DFA individual cap $3M less deductible; deductible greater of $2,500 or 20%. Reported (CBC/National Observer). https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/manitoba/flood-recovery-grant-program-details-9.7277305
- Total provincial cost for the June storms could approach about $300M (as in 2022), per Kinew. $2.2M paid to individuals and $23.1M to the public sector at the time of the report (CBC, date not captured). Reported. https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/manitoba/families-await-spring-flood-funding-9.7407693
- Highway repair cost figures for Hwy 10/Hwy 20 2026: not found. Culvert/bridge tender values: not found.
- Minegoziibe-specific ISC/EMAP funding: not found.
