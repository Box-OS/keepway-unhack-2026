No storm name, and no tropical or post-tropical classification, was found in any source. Treat it as an unnamed slow-moving rain system unless the Canadian Hurricane Centre says otherwise.

Event context (Reported): local states of emergency in Inverness, Richmond and Victoria counties from 2026-09-05. Inverness and Victoria lifted theirs 2026-09-08; Richmond's remained. At the peak, >120 roads were closed or damaged, about 200 culverts were washed out, 8 bridges were damaged and 2 need replacement, and 25 roads were still closed on 2026-09-08. One fatality (a 46-year-old woman, Richmond County). Route 4 was badly damaged between Port Hawkesbury and St. Peter's, there were washouts near St. Peter's and River Bourgeois, and the Hwy 104 km 303–313 flooding closure applied. NS Public Works gave no cost estimate. (globalnews 12050821; yourtricounties)

### 3. Matle'n's Brook / road / elevation
- Matle'n's Brook catchment area and description: **not found** (no geonames hit; likely a local Mi'kmaw name).
- Culvert dimensions or count at the site: **not found**.
- Road layout: Sitmuk Awti / Simtuk Awti (Chapel Island Road) leaves Trunk 4 and ends at the mainland ferry wharf to Chapel Island (IAAC project 81702). The new waterline and sanitary sewer main for the Rapid Housing Subdivision connect to Simtuk Awti. That project has 9 lots of about 1,300 m², a 150 m x 20 m gravel road with ditches and culverts, and a total area of about 15,000 m² (IAAC doc 143267, 2022-03-21, Reported/primary federal notice). This is consistent with the claim that water and wastewater mains lie under the road corridor.
- Road elevation: **not found**. Given that the road ends at a lakeshore wharf, the low point is probably within a few metres of lake level (Estimated, unverified; check NS DEM / LiDAR at nsgi.novascotia.ca).
- Bras d'Or tidal range: about 15 cm near Baddeck and "almost imperceptible" elsewhere, versus about 1.7 m in Chedabucto Bay and about 1.5 m outside the St. Peter's lock. Wind and pressure dominate water levels (Port Hawkesbury Reporter; Yachting World; DFO WebTide model_brasdor). https://porthawkesburyreporter.com/?p=18379. Class: Reported. Conclusion: the tidal range is small (≈0.1–0.2 m), so storm surge and setup matter more than tide.
