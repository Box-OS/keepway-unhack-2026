KEEPWAY

Route, cost and schedule software for road builders that designs roads to stay open in floods, built from an engineering study of four First Nations whose only road keeps washing out.

Start hereKeepway PlannerBest and alternative routes on a flood map, with cost and completion date. Try Scenarios 1, 2 and 3. ToolCrossing screenerEnter failing road crossings and get the cheapest fix, priced and ranked into a capital plan. ToolFlood simulatorPush a flood up and watch today's road close while the fixed crossing stays open.

Working prototype. The Planner runs on generated demo terrain and accepts real elevation data (ESRI ASCII grid). Costs are screening estimates in 2026 CAD (−50% / +100%), not quotes or stamped designs. Saving projects needs the hosted product; here, edits stay in your browser.
